# Contributing to tomi.u

Thank you for your interest in tomi.u!

At this stage, the project is not yet accepting external contributions. Until tomi.u reaches **Phase 2** (see [docs/ROADMAP.md](docs/ROADMAP.md)), development is handled internally and no pull requests or contributions are needed.

Please check back once Phase 2 is underway — contributions will be very welcome at that point!

In the meantime, feel free to:
- Star the repository to follow progress
- Open issues to report bugs or share ideas
- Read the [language specification](docs/language-specification.md) and [ROADMAP](docs/ROADMAP.md)
